# Credential policy

Version: VAS-2026.1  
Owner: Voice AI Space  
Contact: tbot@voiceaispace.com

## Credential ladder

| Level | Public title | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Voice AI Fundamentals | Knowledge Badge |
| 1 | Voice AI Foundations | Knowledge Certificate |
| 2 | Voice AI Practitioner | Professional Certification |
| 3 | Voice AI Architect | Professional Certification |

Each credential is earned independently. Holding a higher level does not imply
that the lower levels were attempted.

## Assessment standard

- 25 scored multiple-choice questions.
- 35-minute limit.
- 80% required to pass.
- 90% or higher is recorded as distinction.
- Unanswered questions are scored as incorrect.
- No negative marking.
- Two attempts per level in a rolling 30-day period.
- The blueprint selects 5 Fundamentals, 6 Architecture, 6 Orchestration,
  4 UX & Design, and 4 Enterprise & Compliance questions.
- Candidate identity is authenticated before the session begins.
- Questions are sampled from the approved bank for the recorded exam version.
- Correct answers and rationales are never delivered to the candidate browser.

## Item approval

An approved item must include:

- a stable private question key;
- one unambiguous prompt;
- exactly four distinct answer options;
- one correct option;
- a rationale explaining the judgment;
- source notes sufficient for review;
- an identified author;
- a different identified reviewer;
- a valid review timestamp;
- the correct version, level, and domain classification.

Items that do not satisfy every field remain drafts and cannot be selected.

## Issuance

Passing candidates receive one credential bound to their authenticated account
and chosen display name. The credential records the assessment version, score,
percentage, level, issue date, and expiry date. Credentials expire 730 days
after issuance.

## Revocation and correction

Voice AI Space may revoke a credential when it was issued in error, obtained
through material misconduct, associated with an invalidated assessment result,
or affected by a security incident. A public revoked credential returns an
invalid status and a reason. Corrections must preserve an audit trail.

## Appeals

Candidates may report an ambiguous or inaccurate item to
tbot@voiceaispace.com. Appeals should identify the attempt date, credential or
session identifier, and the concern without requesting disclosure of the answer
bank. Material scoring corrections must be applied consistently to every
affected attempt.

## Interpretation

The credential attests to performance on a versioned knowledge assessment. It
does not attest to employment history, supervised professional experience,
regulated authorization, academic credit, or guaranteed job performance.
